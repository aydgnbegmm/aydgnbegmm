### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Mechatronics Engineer** specializing in **Embedded Software**, **Autonomous Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAVs)**, and **Robotics Integration**. I build reliable, mission-critical software bridging hardware interfaces (SPI, I2C, UART, CAN), edge compute platforms (Radxa Rock 5B, Raspberry Pi 5), and flight controllers (Pixhawk / ArduPilot).

* 🏆 **Achievements:** 1st Place at **METU VTOL 2023 & 2025**, Finalist at **TEKNOFEST Combat UAV 2024 & 2025**, 1st Place at **TEKNOFEST Aerospace Entrepreneurship 2023**.
* 🔬 **Research:** Principal Researcher for **TÜBİTAK 2209-A** (*Smart Access IoT Lock Mechanism*) & Defense Industry AI Talent Clustering (**SAYZEK ATP**).
* 🎯 **Focus Areas:** Embedded Linux, Real-Time Operating Systems, Sensor Fusion (Kalman Filtering), MAVLink Avionics Protocols, and Edge AI Computer Vision (YOLOv8 + OpenCV).
